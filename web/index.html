Online narrative mapping

We map what's being said online and spot when it's being pushed, not shared.

  1. 10,000 public Bluesky posts are sampled at random from a fixed dataset.
  2. Similar posts are grouped using their text embeddings. Each ball is one group: its area shows the number of posts and its colour shows concern.
  3. Nemotron Lightning describes the framing of groups. Repeated text and other spread signals help identify candidates for human review; a political topic alone is not evidence of propaganda.
  4. Ask Nemotron 3.5 Super about a post, file, image or article. It assesses propaganda-style persuasion in the original material, while a separate judge reviews its relationship to the closest group.
